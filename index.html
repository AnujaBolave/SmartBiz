🧾 Receive stock from supplier bill

Take a photo of the bill or upload an image. SmartBiz reads the text so you don't have to type every product.

Upload a clear JPG/PNG photo. OCR needs an internet connection the first time. Bills have different layouts, so always check the extracted product, quantity and unit price before updating stock.

Step 1: load a bill (photo or demo). Step 2: check and edit every row. Step 3: confirm. Stock changes only at step 3.

📥 Bulk import products

Add many products together using a CSV file exported from Excel or Google Sheets.

Use the template, fill one product per row, then upload the CSV. Required columns: Product Name, Current Stock, Average Sold Per Day, Purchase Price, Target Stock. Selling Price and Expiry Date are optional. Existing products with the same name will be updated.

Tip: In Excel, save your sheet as CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited).
